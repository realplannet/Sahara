The Founder

Neny Trisnayanti

Founder & Master Therapist

Born in Ubud, Bali — the spiritual and healing heartland of Balinese culture — Neny grew up immersed in the ancient traditions of Balinese wellness. From age sixteen, she apprenticed under master healers, learning the art of touch, the language of the body, and the power of botanical medicine.

At twenty-two, her career took her to the world stage — first to luxury resorts in Bali, then to five-star properties across Europe and Asia. In 2008, she arrived in the UAE, where she spent the next decade and a half serving the most discerning guests at Dubai's finest hotels.

In 2018, Neny founded Sahara Balinese Spa with a single vision: to bring that same immaculate, intimate wellness experience directly to clients in their own space. Every Sahara therapist is personally trained and certified by Neny using her proprietary five-star protocol.

What Drives Us

The Sahara Philosophy

I

Authenticity

Every technique we practice traces back to real Balinese tradition. We use genuine Balinese ritual products — volcanic ash, ylang-ylang, coconut oil, turmeric — not generic imitations. What we deliver is the real thing.

II

Excellence

Every therapist undergoes Neny's personal certification programme. We don't cut corners. Protocols, timing, pressure, product application — all are held to the standard of the finest spa floors in the world.

III

Discretion

Uniformed, background-verified professionals. Full liability insurance. Privacy is a core operating principle — not an afterthought. Your space and your trust are treated with the same respect as any five-star property.
